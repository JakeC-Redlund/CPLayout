# Retained drawing review

The original response records and their figure bytes are preserved in a protected
local recovery archive. They are excluded from the public source checkpoint.
This summary contains derived requirements, not verbatim answers or a portable
receipt. Tests use explicit synthetic responses rather than private human input.

The Q01-Q10 review requested rework. Subsequent V01-V04 requirements concerned
bottom-centered drawing and capture actions, horizontal narrow-screen controls,
and distinguishable error, warning and success states. Implementation and human
acceptance remain separate; see the drawing response plan for their disposition.

The later field review completed at revision 16 and drawing review at revision
19. Both were exported with all four annotated figures per packet and verified
against the stored evidence identities. The feedback requests simpler language,
feet and acres, and changes to drawing classification; pause/resume was accepted.
These outcomes do not establish native behavior, field accuracy or release approval.

Originals, prior revisions, source figures, exports, hashes and cleanup evidence
remain privately recoverable. A source checkout does not include those receipts
and must not claim that merely reading this summary verifies their bytes.
